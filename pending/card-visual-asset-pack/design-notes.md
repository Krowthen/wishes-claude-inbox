# Wishes Card Visual Language — Approved Design Notes

## Status

Approved design anchor for the Wishes card asset pack.

## Core Direction

The card system should feel like a clean rustic fantasy artifact: aged metal bindings mounted to dark wood or book-cover material, with selective organic ornament and clear structural geometry. Ornament should feel handcrafted and storied, never busy.

## Primary Leaf Emblem

- Three-leaf composition.
- One vertically dominant center leaf.
- Two lower side leaves open outward in balanced symmetry.
- Pronounced central veins and smaller engraved side veins.
- Lower stems converge into a pointed base.
- Two mirrored curling tendrils form the lower flourish.
- Material: aged brass/bronze with warm highlights and subtle green/brown patina.
- Backing: dark hammered/aged metal or dark wood inset.
- Preferred presentation: circular/oval medallion when used as a seal or card focal point.
- Readability at small size is more important than micro-detail.

### Theme

Nature, growth, continuity, unity, old-world craftsmanship.

## Border Buckle / Crest

- Structural clasp/binding visual for the lower card frame.
- Broad low-profile shield/pentagonal plate with a subtle peaked center.
- Central faceted diamond/leaf-like geometric symbol.
- Restrained mirrored curl/leaf filigree around the central mark.
- Aged bronze/brass edge frame over a darker inset.
- Small rivet details may be used sparingly.
- The buckle should look physically attached to the card/book binding.

## Material Palette

- Dark brown wood / leather-book tones.
- Bronze and aged brass as the main metal family.
- Warm gold only as highlight, not as a bright polished finish.
- Deep brown/near-black recesses for depth.
- Subtle desaturated green patina is acceptable on metal and leaf details.
- Light parchment/ivory is reserved for the front-card information field.

## Surface Treatment

- Light wear on raised edges.
- Fine scratches and age marks.
- Subtle oxidation/patina.
- Darkened recesses.
- No excessive grime, corrosion, or damage that reduces legibility.

## Card Front Rules

- Outer dark wood frame with aged metal bindings.
- Strong corner protection/binding pieces.
- Leaf emblem/medallion centered at the top.
- Buckle/crest centered at the bottom.
- Side strips may carry small leaf/vine accents.
- Keep the main parchment field large and clean for card artwork/content/UI.
- Maintain strong symmetry and a premium crafted-object feel.

## Card Back Rules

- Simple fantasy book-cover composition.
- Preserve the outer binding/corner language from the front.
- Dark wood/leather-book center field remains mostly unornamented.
- One leaf emblem/medallion centered in the card.
- No secondary large crests.
- No additional top/bottom medallions.
- No large vine fields, runes, text, or heraldic clutter.
- The centered leaf is the only dominant focal symbol.

## Usage

These motifs should be reusable independently across:

- card borders / frames
- card backs
- UI panels and dividers
- asset-pack identity elements
- buttons/seals where the leaf medallion fits
- selected fantasy book/interface binding treatments
- ComfyUI visual-reference inputs for consistent Wishes card generation

## Do Not Drift Toward

- bright polished gold
- ornate baroque density
- gothic skull/demon motifs
- high-tech geometry
- excessive Celtic knotwork
- multiple competing centerpieces
- noisy full-surface filigree
- modern flat-vector styling

The visual hierarchy should remain: **structure first, emblem second, ornament third**.
